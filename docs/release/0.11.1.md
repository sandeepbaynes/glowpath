# Glowpath 0.11.1+31

The board now stays in the same pane when an inner foldable display moves between partially open and fully open. Opening gently enlarges and brightens the mission map over 650–850 ms; the puzzle and its controls retain their positions. Reduced motion applies the final appearance immediately.

## Fold layouts

- Book folds keep gameplay on the right and the mission map/countdown on the left.
- Tabletop folds keep gameplay below the hinge. On compact clamshell layouts, the upper half becomes a mission desk: a separate map beside the countdown, with hints and navigation below those instruments.
- The closed iPhone simulator display places the countdown beneath the native time/Wi-Fi region. Its rocket climbs a vertical launch rail. A small orbital beacon uses the camera hole as its visual centre while masking all camera and status exclusions.
- Narrow navigation uses the width of its own pane, preserving 48-point touch targets. No additional page or launch action is introduced.

iOS uses active and inactive UIKit division regions, so a cold launch on an already-open display has the same composition. Android accepts Flutter fold features in both half-open and flat states. A consistent crease gutter prevents changes in interactive margins from recentering the board. Region validation still rejects stale viewport geometry during rotation.

## Verification

**818 Flutter tests pass**, with clean static analysis. The 48 fold-layout cases include closed, book and tabletop layouts, cold-open startup, all three game modes, 4×4/5×5 grids, and 1×/1.5× system text settings. Compact instruments cap their visual text scale at 1.1×; complete countdown status remains available to screen readers.

Tests compare board bounds on the first transition frame, after 16 ms and after settling. Puzzle identity, tile rotations, moves, undo, countdown and saved progress remain unchanged. Vertical-rail and beacon tests cover reduced motion, pause/completion, hit testing and camera/status masking.

Manual simulator review covered closed portrait, book half/open and tabletop half/open. Native inspection confirmed that inactive UIKit division geometry remains available when opening. The reviewed iPhone simulator is the locally installed iPhone Duo profile on iOS 27.2; this is simulator evidence, not physical-device certification.

Nine rendered app captures are reproducible with:

```sh
flutter test tools/render_fold_layout_test.dart --reporter expanded
```

They include synthetic Android clamshell surfaces at 393×852 and 412×915 logical pixels. These exercise the layout contract for Z Flip/Razr-style folds; actual Samsung and Motorola hardware, firmware behavior, cover-screen availability and performance still require device testing. No specific cover-screen compatibility is claimed.
