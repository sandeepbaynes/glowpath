# Glowpath 0.11.3+33

The folded “Signal lost” screen keeps both halves useful. The rocket follows its route, the channel snaps, and the craft and broken pieces drift away in one pane. That scene continues as a quiet distant drift while Retry remains stationary and usable in the other pane. The loss does not replay or reset while waiting for the player.

Reduced motion presents a still broken-route scene immediately. Retrying disposes the scene and returns to the same saved route. The [two-pane layouts from 0.11.2](0.11.2.md) remain in place across the rest of the app.

The free Cloudflare preview package is `glowpath-0.11.3-33-cloudflare-preview-root.zip`. Use the [handoff guide](cloudflare/README.md) on the account machine. Ads, purchases and live shared services remain disabled.

Verification: **885 Flutter tests pass**, with clean static analysis. Nine new sustained-motion tests cover the six-second transition, visible rocket/path at 6, 12 and 60 seconds, fixed Retry bounds, foreground/TickerMode pause and resume, flat/fold transitions, reduced motion and disposal. The actual iPhone simulator was reviewed in book posture after the original loss duration had elapsed. Synthetic flip captures at 6 and 20 seconds are reproducible with `flutter test test/fold_loss_motion_test.dart`; physical Android fold-device testing remains outstanding.
