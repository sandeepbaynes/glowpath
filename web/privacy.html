Privacy

Glowpath is a free puzzle game by Sandeep Baynes. You can play without creating an account. This notice explains the current website and how advertising works in supported mobile builds.

Updated 24 November 2026

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress, scores, collections, earned coins, hint and skip balances, and preferences in your browser or app storage. The website does not sync these saves to a server or another device. Different browsers and website addresses have separate saves.

A small record of recent game events, such as starting a route, using a hint or retrying, is also stored locally. Glowpath does not upload this record to an analytics service. There is no shared account, online leaderboard or hosted player profile in the current website.

When you visit the website

Cloudflare hosts the website and receives the connection information needed to deliver and protect it, including your IP address and request information. This is separate from the game data saved on your device. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its handling of that information.

Fonts and game artwork are served with the game. Glowpath does not ask for contacts, precise location, microphone access or a name to play.

Advertising

The website does not currently load or display ads. Web advertising is pending publisher approval and integration. We will update this notice and provide the relevant privacy choices before activating it.

Supported iPhone, iPad and Android builds may use Google AdMob when advertising is enabled. Google may process device and app information, IP addresses, advertising identifiers where available, and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Privacy choices are presented where required, and a privacy-options control is available when Google requires one. Read Google’s advertising information and privacy policy.

Watching a rewarded ad is optional. Your game progress is preserved when an ad is unavailable or dismissed. Test builds and registered test devices can show test ads instead of live advertising.

Removing your saved data

You can erase web progress by clearing this website’s data in your browser settings. This also removes local preferences and the local event record, and cannot be undone. Removing the mobile app’s data removes its local saves. Glowpath does not hold a server copy of the current website’s progress.

Questions and support

Use the support page for help or questions about this notice. GitHub issues are public; do not include personal information, passwords, advertising identifiers or information about children in a report.
