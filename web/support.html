Flight notes

Find your route.

Draw from the cyan START, collect three stars and reach the gold FINISH before launch. Glowpath is free to play and needs no account.

Move through the puzzle

Drag across adjacent tiles to align a route, or tap a tile to rotate it. Locked paths and other obstacles can limit a turn. Retrace your route to change direction, use Undo to reverse your last move, or ask for a hint when you are stuck. The question-mark control in the game opens more flight notes.

Keep your progress

Use the same browser and website address to continue your voyage. Progress is saved on that device and does not yet sync to another browser or phone. Private browsing, clearing website data or changing to a different game address can start a separate save.

Something went wrong?

Reload once if the game did not finish loading. If the problem continues, report the device and browser, the planet or route, and the steps that led to it. Describe what you expected to happen.

Report an issue on GitHub ↗

Reports are public. Please leave out passwords, personal information and device or advertising identifiers.

About the project

Glowpath is designed by Sandeep Baynes and developed with AI coding agents, with the experience refined through hands-on playtesting. You can follow its development and read the device design decisions in the public repository.
